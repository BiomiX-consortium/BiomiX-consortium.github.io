About

The BiomiX Consortium

Our mission is to make multi-omics analysis accessible to all — building an open platform where researchers can benefit from advanced integration methods without requiring specialized programming expertise.

Who we are

A multi-institution European network of bioinformaticians, data scientists, and domain experts united by a common goal: democratizing multi-omics analysis.
